What is GunBros?

A small artillery game for the browser, made for two brothers in different cities. Chibi war machines take turns lobbing shells across hand-painted, fully destructible maps. Whoever reads the wind best goes home with the win.

  • Turn-based artillery

    One shot per turn, then everyone watches it fly. Every blast carves the ground, so the map changes as you play.

  • Wind and angles

    Set the angle, hold to charge the power bar, and lean into a wind that shifts every couple of turns.

  • 2–8 players online

    Make a room, send the five-letter code or the link, and play 1v1 up to 4v4. Drop out and you can rejoin mid-match.

  • FIRE

    Plays on phones

    Turn your phone sideways: big FIRE and angle controls, drag to look around. Add it to your home screen and it runs full screen.

  • Sky events: Thor, Tornado, Force
  • 8 items in 6 slots
  • Shields, classes and damage types
  • A super shot to charge

mobiles

Every mobile has three shots: two for everyday use and a super shot (SS) that needs a full gauge. Mechanical, bionic and shielded hulls take each damage type differently, so the pick matters.

maps

Painted landscapes where what you see is what you hit: windmills, bridges and temple walls all come down.

How to play

  1. Angle

    Up and down (or drag the dial) raise the barrel. The dial shows your angle against the hull and against the world, since slopes tilt you.

  2. Power

    Hold Space or FIRE to charge, let go to shoot. A marker keeps your last shot, so next turn you can nudge it.

  3. Wind

    The arrow at the top is the wind: direction and strength. It carries every shell and changes every two turns.

  4. Turn order

    Big shots and long turns add delay; the lowest delay goes next. Play fast and light and you might shoot twice in a row.

  5. Items

    Load six slots before the match: Dual, Teleport, Med Kit, Power Up, Wind Change and more. One item per turn.

  6. Super shot

    S1 and S2 are always ready. SS charges a pip each turn and each time you are hit. Last team standing wins.
